### ✨ What's New & Fixed in v0.1.5:
1. **Separate Windows & Android Clipboard History Tabs**:
   - Filter your clipboard timeline into **🌐 All**, **💻 Windows**, or **📱 Android** tabs with live count indicators.
   - Each history item card displays distinctive platform source badges (`💻 Windows` in blue / `📱 Android` in green).
2. **Dedicated Profile Icons for Windows and Android on Network**:
   - Devices on the local network now have clear high-fidelity icons and badges:
     - `💻 Windows PC` (Blue accent)
     - `📱 Android Phone` (Green accent)
   - Top Header displays your active device profile avatar and name with 1-tap profile quick switch.
