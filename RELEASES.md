### ✨ What's New in v0.1.2:
1. **Live Camera QR Code Scanner on Mobile (APK)**:
   - Point your phone camera directly at the PC screen QR code to auto-fill the server IP, authenticate, and connect in under 1 second.
   - Includes camera flip controls and animated neon laser viewfinder.
2. **AES-256-GCM End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)**:
   - Full client-side payload encryption for clipboard and data transfers across Wi-Fi and Bluetooth networks.
   - Zero-trust security: even on open/public Wi-Fi, transfers cannot be intercepted or read by eavesdroppers.
3. **"Drop-to-Device" Visual Drag & Drop Sharing**:
   - Active paired peer devices appear as visual avatar cards.
   - Drag any document, image, or video directly onto a target device bubble to send it instantly.
